## Framework

FeatureGraph is a deterministic framework for transforming ordered observations into explicit behavioral objects. It separates raw observations, sample-level states and events, and temporally bounded objects so that behaviors implicit in a signal become identifiable, measurable, and computationally queryable.

## Problem formulation

Behavioral structures exist within timeseries signals, but they are implicit and often not accessible to other computational entities. Imagine a scientist who had to formulate the laws of physics every time they wanted to carry out a calculation on whether or not a vehicle was accelerating too fast. Such computation would be inaccessible, inconvenient, and often effectively impossible in the time allowed. 

Being able to write down the laws of physics, and not only to write them down but to encode them in computational processes, allows for scientists and other entities to have fast and automated access to information that can be accessed through reusable mathematical structuring.
